# SABIR

> A memecoin on NEAR that you cannot buy, only wait for. Sabir is Turkish for patience.

SABIR is a game and a token on NEAR testnet. You walk a five-minute bridge in the browser. The walk reads how you behave and draws your soul as an NFT. Your 1 NEAR entry stays inside the soul as your own deposit. From then on every living soul earns 1 SABIR a day.

- Leaving early costs an exit tax that falls from 50% to 5% over forty days. The tax stays in a shared vault, so those who leave early pay those who stay.
- Souls change hands only through the contract's own market. Every sale pays 2% to the vault and 1% to the creator. A sold soul loses half its age.
- Any soul can be broken for its deposit. Every broken soul is written to a public exit book on chain.
- The contract has no admin functions. The creator can only change the address the pictures are served from.
