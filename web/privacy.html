SABIR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SABIR is a game and a token on NEAR testnet, made by Heylogram. In short: you have no account with us, there are no cookies and no analytics. What you do on the bridge is written on a public blockchain.

What the walk writes on chain

While you walk the five-minute bridge, the page turns how you move, wait and choose into five scores and a title for your soul. When you pass the gate, these and your NEAR account name are written on NEAR testnet. Anything on a blockchain is public and cannot be deleted, by us or by anyone else. If you do not want this, do not walk the bridge with a wallet; the practice walk without a wallet sends nothing.

Your wallet

You connect a NEAR wallet of your choice, for example MyNearWallet, Meteor or HERE. Each wallet is run by its own maker under its own policy. We never see your recovery words or private keys, and we cannot move your funds.

What stays in your browser

The page keeps the state of a walk in your browser's session storage, so a reload does not lose it. It is gone when you close the tab. There are no cookies and no tracking or analytics scripts.

Our servers

sabir.heylogram.com is served from Heylogram's server. Like any web server it keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, the address requested) to keep the service running and safe. The soul pictures come from api.heylogram.com, which only reads the public chain.

Children

SABIR is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: sabir@heylogram.com
