Test network, no real value

This version runs only on NEAR testnet. Testnet NEAR and SABIR have no money value and cannot be exchanged for real money. The network may reset testnet accounts and balances.

How the game works

  • Entering the bridge costs 1 testnet NEAR. It stays inside your soul as your own deposit.
  • Every living soul earns 1 SABIR a day.
  • Leaving early costs an exit tax that falls from 50% to 5% over forty days. The tax stays in a shared vault, so those who leave early pay those who stay.
  • Souls change hands only through the contract's own market. Every sale pays 2% to the vault and 1% to the creator, and a sold soul loses half its age.
  • Any soul can be broken for its deposit. Every broken soul is written to a public exit book on chain.

These rules are enforced by the smart contract, not by us. The contract has no admin functions; the creator can only change the address the pictures are served from.
